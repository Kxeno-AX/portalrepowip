The Portal Repository: Cosmos

The Portal Repository is a catalog of player-submitted portal addresses in No Man’s Sky. Addresses are categorized by game platform, mode, galaxy and keywords.

Each galaxy contains a network of portals (similar to Stargates) that allow instantaneous travel between worlds within a galaxy.

Portals can be activated by dialing a twelve-digit sequence (out of the sixteen total glyphs). You can learn more about portals on the No Man’s Sky Gamepedia wiki page.
